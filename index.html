Reading the leaderboard

  • Hard by designThe challenges are meant to be out of reach for today's models, so low scores are the expected result. A model that starts scoring well deserves a close look.
  • Small sampleFew challenges, few attempts. A score of 11/18 could mean a true solve rate anywhere from 39% to 80%, so models often share a rank until more attempts separate them.
